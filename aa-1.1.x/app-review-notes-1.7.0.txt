Studio context (please read before evaluating under Guideline 4.3):

This app, Airline Architect, has been LIVE and approved on this account continuously through versions 1.0 to 1.6.0. This submission (1.7.0) is a content update — a scheduled aircraft-maintenance program, new city artwork for 40 airports, and quality-of-life additions. No new purchase; the app's identity is unchanged.

Airline Architect is part of the Architect series by Postmark Digital, a catalog of individually designed simulation games, each modeling a different real-world domain. The series shares a deliberate studio design language (our house UI style and typography), as any established studio's titles share a recognizable visual identity. Each title is an independent game with its own domain simulation, mechanics, content, and data — none is a reskin or a template.

Approved titles under this same account (verifiable): Airline Architect — App ID 6790569697 (this app); FC Architect — App ID 6798026159 (football club management).

What is unique to THIS app: an original airline-operations simulation with no counterpart among our titles — a gravity-based passenger-demand model across ~385 real-world airports (real coordinates, runway lengths, traffic); 37 real aircraft types with individual range, capacity, and economics; distance-based fares and per-type fuel-intensity operating costs; a hub-and-spoke network effect; real FAA Part 117 crew duty/rest limits; cycle-based airframe aging with a scheduled A/C/D maintenance program (new this version) carrying real deferral penalties; seasonal disruptions with real airport night curfews and a day/night terminator; region-aware competitor airlines flying real fleets; and a late-game capital layer — loans, going public (a live share price with activist investors and a board that can vote you out), and acquiring rivals. None of these appears in any sibling title. We share a studio codebase and design system across our own titles — studio infrastructure, not a third-party template — and every simulation system is our original work.

Single-player airline-management simulation. No account or login — the app opens directly into the game, so no demo credentials are needed.

IN-APP PURCHASE (ONE-TIME UNLOCK): Free to play with the full feature set; the free tier limits network SIZE (max 6 aircraft, max 5 open routes), not features. A single NON-CONSUMABLE purchase ("Full Game Unlock", $9.99 founding, regular $19.99) removes both limits permanently — one-time, not a subscription. The purchase screen shows the price, "Restore Purchase", and functional Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links. (Two legacy auto-renewable subscriptions remain active for existing subscribers only, not offered for sale here.) Reach it from the Finance tab's "FREE PLAN" card, or when a limit is hit.

GAME CENTER: 29 achievements + 2 leaderboards. Sign-in is optional. Achievements report as milestones are earned and are viewable in Apple's Games app; there is deliberately no in-app Game Center browser UI (intentional, not incomplete).

TESTING NOTES: The sim runs continuously — use the speed control at the bottom of the Network tab (up to 100x) to advance time. Aircraft need crew: if one sits idle at a gate, hire crew in the Crews tab (crews follow real duty/rest limits, so one crew cannot fly continuously — intended). New in 1.7: the Ops tab's "MX" section lists aircraft due for scheduled checks; Details lets you service, cover the route with a spare, or suspend it. Real airports, aircraft types, and airline names appear as text-only reference data (no logos, liveries, or branding; no affiliation implied).

PRIVACY: no personal data, no ad SDKs, no tracking. Anonymous aggregate analytics via TelemetryDeck (no identifiers); RevenueCat verifies purchase status only. Saves sync across the user's own devices via iCloud; no game data reaches us and there is no server.
