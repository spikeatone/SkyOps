Studio context (please read before evaluating under 4.3):

This app has been LIVE and approved on this account continuously from 1.0 through 1.9.0. This submission (1.10) is a feature update to that same app — route planning, a saved-plans list, and maintenance-base upgrades. No new purchase; identity, pricing and business model unchanged.

Airline Architect is part of the Architect series by Postmark Digital — a catalog of individually designed simulation games, each modeling a different real-world domain. They share only a studio design language (our house UI style and typography), the way any studio's titles share a visual identity; each is an independent game with its own simulation, mechanics and data, not a reskin or a template. Other approved titles on this same account (verifiable): FC Architect (App ID 6798026159, football management) and Vineyard Architect (winegrowing).

This app's simulation is its own: a gravity-based passenger-demand model across ~385 real airports, 37 aircraft types with individual economics, FAA Part 117 crew limits with a training pipeline, scheduled A/C/D maintenance, region-aware competitors, and a late-game capital layer. Every system is our original work.

Single-player airline-management simulation. No account or login — the app opens straight into the game, so no demo credentials are needed.

IN-APP PURCHASE (ONE-TIME UNLOCK): Free to play with the full feature set; the free tier limits network SIZE (6 aircraft, 5 routes), not features. One NON-CONSUMABLE purchase ("Full Game Unlock", $9.99 founding / $19.99 regular) removes both limits permanently — not a subscription. The purchase screen shows the price, "Restore Purchase", and working Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links. (Two legacy subscriptions remain active for existing subscribers only, not sold here.) Reach it from the Finance tab's "FREE PLAN" card, or when a limit is hit.

GAME CENTER: 29 achievements + 2 leaderboards, sign-in optional. Achievements report as milestones are earned and appear in Apple's Games app; there is deliberately no in-app Game Center browser UI (intentional, not incomplete).

TESTING NOTES: The sim runs continuously — use the speed control at the bottom of the Network tab (up to 100x) to advance time. NEW route planning: on the Network tab tap "Open Route", then tap two airports — a research panel now shows distance, runway, demand and projected economics BEFORE any aircraft is bought. "Assign an aircraft" commits it; "Save to plans" parks it (free) in a Planned drawer under "Routes". "Add a stop" extends it into a multi-city loop. Maintenance is under its own Fleet segment: build a line station or hangar base at a hub or any 3-route airport, and a line station can be upgraded to a hangar for the price difference. Crew must be trained before flying: a "Rated hire" is line-ready in ~10 sim-days, a "New hire" in ~45, so newly hired crew is deliberately NOT available immediately — intended, not a hang. Real airports, aircraft types and airline names are text-only reference data (no logos, liveries or branding; no affiliation implied).

PRIVACY: no personal data, no ad SDKs, no tracking. Anonymous aggregate analytics via TelemetryDeck (no identifiers); RevenueCat verifies purchase status only. Saves sync across the user's own devices via iCloud; no game data reaches us, and we run no server.
