Studio context (please read before evaluating under 4.3):

This app has been LIVE and approved on this account continuously through 1.0 to 1.7.0. This submission (1.8.0) is a feature update to that same app — crew training, multi-city routing, and a relocated maintenance section. No new purchase; identity, pricing and business model unchanged.

Airline Architect is part of the Architect series by Postmark Digital, a catalog of individually designed simulation games, each modeling a different real-world domain. They share a studio design language — our house UI style and typography — as any studio's titles share a visual identity. Each is an independent game with its own simulation, mechanics, content and data — not a reskin or a template.

Approved titles on this same account (verifiable): Airline Architect — App ID 6790569697 (this app); FC Architect — App ID 6798026159 (football management); Vineyard Architect (winegrowing).

Unique to THIS app, with no counterpart among our titles: a gravity-based passenger-demand model across ~385 real airports (real coordinates, runway lengths, traffic); 37 real aircraft types with individual range, capacity and economics; distance-based fares and per-type fuel-intensity costs; a hub-and-spoke network effect; real FAA Part 117 crew duty/rest limits, extended this version into a training pipeline with type ratings, recurrent currency and a player-built simulator facility; multi-city rotations (new); cycle-based airframe aging with scheduled A/C/D maintenance carrying real deferral penalties; real airport night curfews; region-aware competitors flying real fleets from their real hubs; and a late-game capital layer — loans, going public (live share price, activists, a board that can remove you), and acquiring rivals. We share a studio codebase and design system across our own titles — studio infrastructure, not a third-party template — and every simulation system is our original work.

Single-player airline-management simulation. No account or login — the app opens straight into the game, so no demo credentials are needed.

IN-APP PURCHASE (ONE-TIME UNLOCK): Free to play with the full feature set; the free tier limits network SIZE (6 aircraft, 5 routes), not features. One NON-CONSUMABLE purchase ("Full Game Unlock", $9.99 founding / $19.99 regular) removes both limits permanently — not a subscription. The purchase screen shows the price, "Restore Purchase", and working Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links. (Two legacy subscriptions remain active for existing subscribers only, not sold here.) Reach it from the Finance tab's "FREE PLAN" card, or when a limit is hit.

GAME CENTER: 29 achievements + 2 leaderboards, sign-in optional. Achievements report as milestones are earned and appear in Apple's Games app; there is deliberately no in-app Game Center browser UI (intentional, not incomplete).

TESTING NOTES: The sim runs continuously — use the speed control at the bottom of the Network tab (up to 100x) to advance time. Aircraft need crew, and in 1.8 crew must be TRAINED first: in the Crews tab, "Rated hire" is line-ready in ~10 sim-days and "New hire + type rating" ~45, so a newly hired crew is deliberately NOT available immediately — intended, not a hang. The Chief Pilot card there says whether a shortage is real or just training in progress. Multi-city routes: Network tab, "Open Route", pick an aircraft, then tap 2-5 airports in order. Maintenance moved this version from Ops to its own segment under Fleet; Ops keeps a one-line summary linking there. Real airports, aircraft types and airline names are text-only reference data (no logos, liveries or branding; no affiliation implied).

PRIVACY: no personal data, no ad SDKs, no tracking. Anonymous aggregate analytics via TelemetryDeck (no identifiers); RevenueCat verifies purchase status only. Saves sync across the user's own devices via iCloud; no game data reaches us, and we run no server.
